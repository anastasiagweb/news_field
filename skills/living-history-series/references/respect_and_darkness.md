# Respect and Darkness — How Hard, How Honest, How Respectful

Read before series design, story cards, frameworks, and every respect audit. The line writes about hundreds of civilisations, many with living descendants and living religions. It writes about slavery, war, sacrifice, hunger, and death because they were there. This document sets the ceiling for darkness and the floor for respect.

---

## Audience

Adult readers — native speakers and adult learners. Books should also be safe to give to a thoughtful older teenager. That sets the ceiling.

## The darkness ceiling

**Shown honestly:**
- Slavery and unfreedom as daily reality: work, sale, punishment, separation of families, escape, release.
- War and raiding: fear, flight, siege, loss, captivity.
- Death in every form the period knew.
- Hunger, disease, disaster, poverty, debt.
- Harsh law and harsh customs of the period.

**How it is shown:**
- Violence happens **mostly off the page**: before and after, sounds, consequences, faces. A blow may land on the page; wounds are described briefly and without lingering.
- **No gore**, no torture on the page, no lingering on suffering for effect.
- **Ritual killing and sacrifice of humans**, only where attested for the window, shown from inside the culture's meaning, with restraint, never as spectacle; never invented where not attested.
- **Sexual content:** none beyond the implied. Marriage, desire, love, adultery, prostitution, and sexual exploitation of the unfree may exist in the story world; they are treated with discretion, never described, never eroticised. No sexual content involving minors in any form, including implication.
- **Children** may live the realities of their world (work, illness, loss, slavery), never shown as victims of violence on the page.
- **Animals:** sacrifice, hunting, and slaughter were everyday; shown plainly and briefly.

**Not allowed:** cruelty as entertainment; shock endings for their own sake; a story whose main pleasure is suffering.

## The respect floor

1. **From the inside.** Every culture is written from the inside, with the dignity of its own people. No framing as primitive, savage, barbaric, exotic, mysterious, or strange — in narration or in Notes.
2. **No idealisation either.** No peaceful utopias where evidence shows conflict; no imagined social paradises where the evidence is ambiguous.
3. **Beliefs are not jokes.** Characters may joke about their gods as their culture did; the text never mocks the beliefs, and the narrator never calls them superstition.
4. **Living religions and living descendants.** Where a culture's religion or traditions are still practised, the series bible must identify the living communities; avoid portraying sacred figures in ways those communities would find disrespectful; **never reveal restricted or secret-sacred knowledge**, even if some of it has been published; and prefer sources that include the community's own scholarship.
5. **No stereotypes.** Every foreigner, minority, and group is checked for stereotype. Foreigners are individuals.
6. **Enslaved people are people:** with names where the period gave them names, wants, skills, humour, and choices inside their constraints — never props, never only victims.
7. **Modern politics stays out.** Present-day nations, conflicts, and identity politics are not mapped onto the past. Stories use the period's names for peoples and places.

## Real people

Real historical persons appear only as background or brief cameo (`line_doctrine.md`). Never shown committing invented crimes, never given invented private lives, never ridiculed.

## Severity guidance

- **Critical:** gore or torture on the page; sexual content beyond implication; any sexualisation of minors; contempt for a culture or religion; restricted sacred knowledge revealed; a stereotype that defines a group.
- **Major:** violence lingered on; an exoticising narrator; an enslaved character used only as a prop; a minor stereotype; an idealisation contrary to evidence.
- **Minor:** a single exoticising word in narration.

The respect gate in every audit is **zero Critical**; Majors are fixed or carry a recorded accept-as-is rationale.
