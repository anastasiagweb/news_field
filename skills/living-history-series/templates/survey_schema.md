# Survey Schema — `01_survey.md`

Produced by P1. A research document about the whole civilisation, written for the series designers. Every non-trivial claim carries an evidence status and a source key; sources are listed in `01_sources.md` (same table format as the fact-check report's sources).

```
# Civilisation Survey — <civilisation>

## 1. Span and chronology
- Scholarly periodisation table: | Period | Dates (convention; alternatives) | Defining changes | Main sites | Evidence density 1–5 |
- Chronology debates that matter for fiction (what is contested, which convention we propose).

## 2. Geography
- Regions, landscapes, climate, coastlines and their changes, main routes; maps described in words.

## 3. Evidence landscape
- Kinds of evidence per period (texts, archaeology, images, later writers) and their limits.
- What can and cannot be known about ordinary lives in each period.

## 4. Daily-life domains per period
For each period: work, food, housing, clothing, family, religion, law, travel, death — summary with status and keys. Mark what changes between periods.

## 5. People
- Social groups documented per period (including unfree, foreigners, women, children, elders) and what we know of each.
- Names: what onomastic evidence exists per period; sources.

## 6. Mentalité overview
- Beliefs, values, emotions, humour per period, as far as evidence allows; scholarly sources.
```
