Produced by P9, revised by P11, finalised as `pitches/bookNN_<slug>.md`. A book pitch is the direct input of the `living-history-book` skill, together with the bible. Target length: 8,000–12,000 words. Full sentences everywhere; no telegraphic fragments.

# Book <NN> — <Title>

## 1. Book identity
- The world-window in one paragraph: place, dates, social world, what makes it distinct.
- Why these ten stories belong together (shared world, not shared theme).

## 2. Period sheet
### 2.1 Situation in this window
Political, social, economic, religious situation — the essentials every story assumes. Evidence markup as in the bible.
### 2.2 Key facts for this book
20–40 load-bearing facts the stories rely on, with status and source keys.
### 2.3 NOT-YET list for this book
The 30+ anachronism traps most likely in these ten stories, with earliest attestation and keys.
### 2.4 Rendering choices
Terms, titles, units, gods, places — how this book renders them (extends bible §7).
### 2.5 Names
Names allocated to this book (from the registry) and a reserve of at least 15 more (attested, by sex/status).

## 3. Front and back matter plan
- About this book: the three to five things it must orient the reader to; evidence it will name; the note on names if needed.
- Timeline: 5–8 draft entries with status and keys.
- What came next: the facts it will cover, with keys.

## 4. Variety table
The book's completed variety-contract table (as in the slate), updated for any changes since the slate.
